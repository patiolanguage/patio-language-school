Good news

Easier than you think.

Mandarin grammar is surprisingly straightforward. There is far less to memorise than in most European languages, so you can start building real sentences quickly.

No conjugations

A verb never changes. “I go”, “she goes”, “we go”: it is the same word every time.

No verb tenses

Words like “yesterday” or “tomorrow” tell you when. The verb stays the same.

No plurals

One book, three books. The noun does not change.

No gendered nouns

No masculine or feminine to remember. Every noun just is.

The new part is the tones. We practise them from the very first class, out loud and with plenty of laughs, so they soon feel natural.
