Not a classroom. A patio.

Grammar drills only get you so far. At Patio Language School, you learn European Portuguese the way locals actually use it — in conversation, over a coffee, out in the city. Classes are small, personal, and built around real life in Lagos and the Algarve.

Private Lessons

One-on-one sessions built entirely around your pace, your goals, and your schedule.

Small Group Classes

Learn alongside a handful of other students — more conversation, more accountability, lower cost per hour.

Cultural Workshops & Outings

City walks, market visits, and talks that put the language in context — because culture is half the lesson.

Rooted in Lagos, built for real life

Whether you've just moved to the Algarve, split your time between here and home, or simply fell in love with Portugal — our classes meet you where you are, in the city that inspired the school.
