About Us

A language and culture space in Lagos.

Patio Language School was created to bring people together through meaningful learning and shared experiences. Reading, writing, listening, and speaking are the foundations of language learning, but we believe language comes alive through conversation, culture, and community.

Our patio is more than a name. It is the idea at the heart of everything we do. A patio is a natural meeting spot, open and unhurried, where people gather and share a coffee, a story, and a laugh. We wanted to build exactly that, a warm, welcoming space where you can learn Portuguese and step into Portuguese culture without fear or hesitation.

Meet the Founders

Built by two people who understand what it's like to live abroad.

Patio Language School was founded by Sofia Nabais and Claire Lehto, who bring together their academic background and real-world experience of learning a new language and building a life in a different culture.

Sofia Nabais

Co-Founder & Director of Studies

Sofia leads Patio's academic programmes, designing courses that are practical, engaging, and rooted in real-life Portuguese. She holds a degree in Mathematics and is certified in pedagogical skills, with many years of teaching experience. Sofia lived in Barcelona and Frankfurt from 2014 to 2023, an experience that gave her a deep, personal understanding of what it takes to master the language of your host country. Since returning to Portugal in 2023, she has dedicated herself to teaching European Portuguese to foreign residents and visitors, drawing on her own experience abroad to help students navigate learning a new language and find their place in Portuguese culture.

Claire Lehto

Co-Founder & Managing Director

Claire leads operations and the overall student experience at Patio, making sure everything feels welcoming and organized. She holds a BA in Cultural Geography from the University of Texas at Austin and a Master of Public Administration from Bowie State University. Having lived in five countries, Claire's path has taken her from serving Girl Scout communities in Alaska, to teaching English online to children in China, to leading educational programmes in US National Parks. That curiosity and cultural fluency now shape Patio's programming and community.
