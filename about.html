About Us

Language lives outside the textbook.

Patio Language School started with a simple idea: the best way to learn European Portuguese is the way you'd naturally pick up any language — through people, place, and conversation.

Why "Patio"?

A patio is where things actually happen — where neighbours talk, coffee gets poured, and stories get told. That's the model for how we teach: less lecture, more conversation. Learning happens over coffee, on walks through Lagos, and in small groups where everyone's part of the conversation.

We're as much a cultural community as a school. Whether you're 28 or 78, newly arrived in the Algarve or years in, you'll find a warm, welcoming place to learn European Portuguese and get to know the culture around it — Portugal's customs, heritage, and everyday life. Alongside private and group lessons, we host cultural gatherings, city outings, and get-togethers that put the language in context: the market, the café, the history behind the tiles on the wall.

Conversation First

Every session is built around real spoken Portuguese, not just grammar tables.

Small & Welcoming

Private lessons and small groups — enough room for everyone to speak, at any age or level.

Rooted in Lagos

Gatherings and outings use the city itself as a classroom and a meeting place.
