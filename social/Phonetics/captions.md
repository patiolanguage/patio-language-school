## Email: students and newsletter list (Fri 16 Oct)

**Subject:** Avô or avó? A pronunciation workshop with Sofia

Olá,

You know the words. You've practised the phrase. And then the person at the café answers you in English.

Usually it isn't your grammar. It's a handful of sounds that European Portuguese does differently: vowels that almost disappear, the nasal "ão", the "s" that turns into "sh", and the lh, nh and r.

Sofia is running a **Phonetics Workshop** to work on exactly these. It's a small group with lots of listening and speaking, and you'll leave knowing what to practise on your own.
